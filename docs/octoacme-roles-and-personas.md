# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with other roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to confirm scope and acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery timing and dependencies.
- Partner with QA to validate quality and fix defects before release.
- Engage Technical Leads for architecture guidance on complex or cross-cutting work.

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with other roles
- Align with Project Managers on milestone planning, sequencing, and communication.
- Collaborate with Developers to prioritize feasible scope and clarify requirements.
- Work with UX/Design Leads to validate customer experience and usability outcomes.
- Support Sponsor/Stakeholder decisions with data and business context.

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with other roles
- Partner with Product Managers on prioritization, milestones, and organizational dependencies.
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Leads to track execution and unblock work.
- Work with QA and Security roles to monitor release readiness and risk status.
- Escalate unresolved issues through Sponsor/Stakeholder channels when necessary.

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads define quality strategy, design test plans, and own acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test strategy and test plans
- Define acceptance criteria quality gates
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Collaborate with developers on testability and edge cases
- Track and triage defects
- Participate in release readiness assessments

### Goals
- Ensure critical defects do not reach production
- Reduce cycle time through automation and parallel testing
- Improve test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- Test results and defect triage in daily standups
- QA sign-off in release readiness checklist

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to identify defects, improve testability, and verify fixes.
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm business requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Supports the Project Manager in tracking release quality risk and readiness.
- Coordinates with Security/Compliance roles on security-focused validation and regression checks.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and risk mitigation. They work across the team to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct technical design reviews
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Guide technology choices and trade-offs
- Mentor developers on best practices
- Collaborate with product on feasibility and scope
- Ensure compliance with technical standards

### Goals
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Improve code quality and maintainability
- Enable faster delivery through architectural clarity

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions during planning
- Code review feedback
- Risk assessments in weekly syncs

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers on implementation approaches, trade-offs, and technical constraints.
- Works with Product Managers to assess scope feasibility and delivery options.
- Supports the Project Manager in identifying cross-team technical dependencies and delivery risks.
- Collaborates with Security/Compliance to ensure technical decisions meet risk and compliance requirements.

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help teams operate effectively, remove impediments, and improve delivery rhythm without sacrificing quality or collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies and working agreements
- Remove blockers and support team flow
- Coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Help maintain focus on priorities and sustainable delivery
- Support retrospectives and action tracking

### Goals
- Improve team velocity without burning out contributors
- Strengthen collaboration, transparency, and accountability
- Create a predictable, healthy delivery process

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint demos
- Retrospective facilitation and improvement planning
- Team health and process conversations with PM and leadership

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Developers and Product Managers by keeping planning, execution, and retrospectives productive.
- Works with Project Managers to improve cadence, risk visibility, and stakeholder alignment.
- Helps Technical Leads and QA drive effective collaboration and quicker issue resolution.
- Shares process insights with Sponsor/Stakeholder groups when delivery improvements affect timelines or priorities.

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security requirements, compliance standards, and risk thresholds. They embed security into the development process.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Review code and architecture for security risks
- Coordinate security scanning in CI/CD
- Manage compliance sign-off and audit trails
- Respond to and triage security incidents

### Goals
- Prevent security incidents and data breaches
- Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
- Build security culture within the team

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements in acceptance criteria
- Code review and architecture review participation
- Security incident playbook and escalation paths

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Leads on secure design and safe implementation patterns.
- Guides Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs, compliance needs, and release controls.
- Partners with QA to validate security checks and incident readiness.
- Escalates issues to Sponsor/Stakeholder stakeholders when business or compliance risk is material.

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads ensure that products are usable, accessible, and aligned to customer needs. They connect user insight, design consistency, and product intent to execution.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX direction, flows, and design standards
- Translate customer needs into clear interaction models
- Partner with product on usability and research-informed decisions
- Review interfaces for accessibility and consistency
- Support design validation across release milestones

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and usability
- Reduce friction in key user journeys
- Deliver consistent, accessible product experiences

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and stakeholder reviews
- User research summaries and usability findings
- Alignment with product and engineering on experience decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer outcomes and prioritize UX improvements.
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure design intent is implemented accurately.
- Supports QA in validating experience quality and accessibility requirements.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design dependencies, risks, or schedule impacts.

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Stakeholders represent business interests, define success criteria, and provide governance oversight. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Provide executive feedback and trade-off decisions
- Monitor project health and escalations
- Communicate outcomes and impact to leadership

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Reduce execution risk through clear governance

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Escalation and risk reporting

### Interaction with existing roles
- Aligns with Product Managers and Project Managers on priorities, funding, and delivery scope.
- Receives updates from QA, Security, and Technical Leads on key risks and release readiness.
- Provides decision-making support when project trade-offs require executive input.
- Works with the broader team to maintain focus on strategic outcomes and accountability.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles help clarify ownership, communication patterns, and decision-making paths across project delivery.
- The team can combine these personas to model cross-functional collaboration, escalation, and accountability in project work.

---

## Summary
The OctoAcme persona model is designed to support clarity, accountability, and communication across the full project lifecycle. When teams understand who owns product decisions, technical strategy, quality, security, user experience, and stakeholder alignment, they are better able to deliver successful outcomes with fewer gaps and less ambiguity.
